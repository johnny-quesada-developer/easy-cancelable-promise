## The code

Both functions do the same work: they count prime numbers in steps, with a pause between steps so the page
stays free. The only difference is the promise they return.

<CodeBlock code={search} lang="ts" title="search.ts" />

The native version has nothing to call. The example can stop listening to it, but the timer that schedules the
next step is out of reach, so every step still runs.

The cancelable version registers one line with `onCancel`. Canceling the promise clears the timer, and the next
step never happens.

<Callout type="note" title="What is measured">
  Steps after Stop counts the steps each promise ran after you pressed the button. Work after Stop is the
  time between the button and the last step of that promise. Both are measured in your browser, on the real
  library.
</Callout>
