The example listens with `onProgress`. It also chains a second promise with `then` and listens on that one: the
reports reach both.

<CodeBlock code={demo} lang="tsx" title="ProgressDemo.tsx" />

<Callout type="note" title="Without the library">
  A native promise can report progress only through something else you pass in: a callback, an event emitter, a
  stream. That second channel has to be carried along with the promise everywhere the promise goes.
</Callout>
